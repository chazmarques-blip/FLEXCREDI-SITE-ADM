Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

What's the difference between credit types?

Each credit modality has specific characteristics to meet different needs. Personal Credit is more flexible and fast, ideal for general needs. Business Credit offers larger amounts and special conditions for entrepreneurs. Debt Consolidation focuses on reorganizing your finances, and Home Improvement Credit has longer terms for house projects.

Can I contract more than one type of credit?

Yes, as long as your payment capacity can handle the commitments. Our analysis considers your total income and existing commitments to ensure you have comfortable conditions to honor the payments.

Can rates change after contracting?

No. All our rates are fixed and defined at the time of contracting. The installment amount remains the same throughout the entire contract period, ensuring total predictability in your budget.

Do I need collateral or co-signers?

In most cases, no. We work mainly with personal credit without collateral. Only in cases of very high amounts or specific profiles we may request additional guarantees, always informing in advance.

What's the approval timeframe?

Analysis and approval happen within minutes in most cases. After approval, money is released within 24 hours for personal credit and consolidation, up to 48h for business and up to 72h for home improvements.

Can I pay early?

Yes! We offer progressive discount for early installment payment or full payoff. The discount is calculated proportionally to the time advanced and you can simulate the value through our app or website.
